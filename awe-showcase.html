About

What is iAWE?

The iAWE (Intelligent Automated Writing Evaluation) system is a multimodal, LLM-based platform designed to provide formative assessment for student writing at the Center for Preparatory Studies, Sultan Qaboos University. It supports five writing courses across the Foundation and Post-Foundation programs, each with tailored rubrics calibrated to CEFR A1–B2 proficiency levels. The system offers two dedicated Progressive Web Apps: the blue iAWE Teacher App with paid API access and secure email-whitelisted authentication for instructors, and the green AWE Student App with free API access for students. A Google Forms-based system is also available for quick, no-installation submission. All systems use Gemini AI-powered OCR to extract text from handwritten essays and evaluate writing against course-specific rubrics, delivering detailed, actionable assessment reports in seconds.

How It Works

Four simple steps from handwritten essay to detailed AI-powered feedback.

Scan & Upload

Students take a photo of their handwritten essay or type directly into the app. Multi-page uploads support essays up to two pages.

AI OCR Processing

Google Gemini AI extracts text from handwritten images with high accuracy, handling varied handwriting styles and layouts.

AI Assessment

Gemini evaluates the extracted text against course-specific, CEFR-aligned rubrics across four criteria with detailed band descriptors.

Detailed Feedback

Students receive scores with justifications, exact error quotes, improvement suggestions, and downloadable PDF reports.

Features

Assessment Features

A comprehensive suite of AI-powered tools designed for accurate, transparent writing evaluation.

Gemini AI Assessment

Google Gemini evaluates writing against course-specific rubrics with detailed band descriptors, ensuring transparent and consistent evaluation.

AI-Powered OCR

Gemini AI extracts text from handwritten essays with high accuracy, handling varied handwriting styles, multi-page uploads, and complex layouts.

Course-Specific Rubrics

Tailored rubrics for each course with CEFR-aligned band descriptors, supporting Foundation (A1–A2) and Post-Foundation (A2–B2) levels.

Error Identification

Exact quotes from the student text are cited alongside explanations, pinpointing specific areas needing improvement with precise evidence.

PDF Assessment Reports

Download comprehensive assessment reports as PDF files, including scores, justifications, errors, and improvement suggestions for record-keeping.

Two Dedicated Apps

Two purpose-built PWAs — the blue iAWE Teacher App with secure email-whitelisted access, and the green AWE Student App with free, no-login access — both installable on any device for instant, camera-based essay scanning.
